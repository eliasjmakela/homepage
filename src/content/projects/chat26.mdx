## What it is

A simple chat app where messages arrive in real time.

## How it's built

The frontend is Next and TypeScript. The backend is Rust, and messages travel
between the two over a WebSocket connection.

## Why I built it

I've wondered how WebSocket works, and I've also thought that Rust was a really interesting language, and building this gave me the opportunity to practice both.
